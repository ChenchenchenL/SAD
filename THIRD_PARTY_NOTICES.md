Its runtime Python sources are included in `data/external/malrulelib_repo`. Its original [LICENSE](data/external/malrulelib_repo/LICENSE) and README are retained. The upstream license permits non-commercial uses under its stated conditions, requires attribution and share-alike distribution, and specifically prohibits training, fine-tuning or evaluating commercial or closed-source LLMs. The SAD license does not override these terms. Upstream generated benchmark CSVs are not included.

Related publication: X. Chen, N. Liu and S. Sonkar, “MalruleLib: Large-Scale Executable Misconception Reasoning with Step Traces for Modeling Student Thinking in Mathematics”, ACL 2026, <https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.690>.
